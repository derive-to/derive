# Harbor Line Tram Renewal

A *synthetic* report on renewing the [harbor loop](https://example.com/harbor-loop), written for the **fuzz harness** and nobody else.  
Every figure here is invented &mdash; the depot on `Pier 9` does not exist.

## Why the line matters

The **harbor loop** carries early crews to the fish market, the ferry terminal, and the *old customs house*. Riders on [route 7](https://example.com/route-7) transfer at `Stop 12` twice a day.

When the harbor loop closes for work, buses replace it. Buses replace it slowly: the detour adds **eleven minutes** each way, and the harbor loop is the only step-free route to the water.

> Keep the harbor loop running on weekends, even if weekday service pauses.

## Phases of the work

1. Survey the track bed and **mark the weak joints**.
2. Replace the rails between the depot and `Stop 12`.
   - Night crews lift the old rail.
   - Day crews lay the new rail.
3. Rewire the overhead line and *test every section*.
4. Reopen the harbor loop with a slow first week.

Each phase ends with a walk of the line.\
Each phase ends with a short note to riders.

## Budget at a glance

| Phase | Crew weeks | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Survey | 4 | Mostly *night* work |
| Rails | 18 | Largest share by far |
| Overhead | 9 | Needs `OL-2` permits |
| Reopening | 2 | Slow first week |

The rails phase is the largest share by far. Everything else fits inside **one season** &amp; nothing spills over.
