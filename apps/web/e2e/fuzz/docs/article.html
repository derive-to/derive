Harbor Line Tram Renewal
Field Notes, Spring Survey

A synthetic report on renewing the harbor loop, written for the fuzz harness and nobody else.

Every figure here is invented. The harbor loop is a made-up line, and the depot on Pier 9 does not exist.

Why the line matters

The harbor loop carries early crews to the fish market, the ferry terminal, and the old customs house. Riders on route 7 transfer at Stop 12 twice a day.

When the harbor loop closes for work, buses replace it. Buses replace it slowly: the detour adds eleven minutes each way, and the harbor loop is the only step-free route to the water.

Keep the harbor loop running on weekends, even if weekday service pauses.

Phases of the work
and what each one needs

  1. Survey the track bed and mark the weak joints.
  2. Replace the rails between the depot and Stop 12.
    • Night crews lift the old rail.
    • Day crews lay the new rail.
  3. Rewire the overhead line and test every section.
  4. Reopen the harbor loop with a slow first week.

Each phase ends with a walk of the line. Each phase ends with a short note to riders.
