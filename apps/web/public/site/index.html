How teams use it

Make active work easy to find and follow

Derive keeps the output of people and agents in one workspace. There is no required process. Publish, review, comment, and iterate in the way that fits the work.

PublishPut the real work online
Publish Markdown, HTML, or a built site. Fonts, scripts, and images stay together in a sandboxed viewer.
FindOpen the current version
Search by name, tag, collection, or content. The workspace library shows what is active and who changed it.
ShareSend one link
Keep work private, share it by link, or protect it with a password. You can change or revoke access later.
DiscussComment on the work itself
Leave feedback on exact text, mention a person or agent, or edit directly. Commenting and editing require sign-in.
UpdateKeep earlier versions intact
Publish again at the same URL. Compare versions, see what changed, and restore one when you need it.
DecideUse formal review when it helps
Most work can move through comments and edits. Recorded approval is there for the work that needs a clear decision.
