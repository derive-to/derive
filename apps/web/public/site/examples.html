FIG. 02 · Use one

Publish with the workspace default.

The example configurations intentionally omit access fields so cloning one never widens visibility without your decision.

git clone https://github.com/derive-to/derive.git
cd derive/examples/technical-plan
derive login
derive publish
Then keep it. Choose the link role you intend, share the URL, and revise in place as the work moves. Comments and formal review are there when a decision needs them; the URL staying current is the part that always matters.
FIG. 03 · Proof standard

What we will and will not call evidence.

Official samples stay labeledIllustrative metrics, companies, and scenarios are never presented as customer results.
Customer stories require consentA real case names the workflow, baseline, revisions, outcome, and limits its participant approved for publication.
A link alone is not adoptionSuccess means the artifact is still current months later: revised in place, at the same URL, with a history that says what changed.
