Privacy

How Derive handles your data

We store the account details, published content, comments, and limited usage data needed to run Derive. We do not sell your data or use ad trackers.

Effective August 10, 2026. This page is a plain-language account of how the hosted service works. Email hello@derive.to if you need more detail.

Data

What we collect and why

Account details
Your email, name, and handle. We use them to sign you in and show who published or commented.
Workspace content
The artifacts you publish, their version history, and the comments people leave on them.
Limited usage data
First-party view analytics for published work, plus a broad signup source carried in the signup link. We do not use attribution cookies or browser storage.
No ad tracking or data sales
We do not use ad trackers or third-party analytics scripts, and we do not sell your data.

Details

Common questions

Who processes data for Derive?

Cloudflare hosts the app and stores artifact files. Neon stores workspace records, comments, and the search index. Stripe processes billing events. Resend sends password resets and notifications. Slack and GitHub receive workspace data only after an owner connects the integration. A third-party model provider receives content only when an optional feature such as chat or automations uses it. Search indexing runs within Cloudflare's infrastructure.

What happens when I delete my account or workspace?

Deleting your account removes your sign-in data, memberships, and personal workspace record. It also removes your name from work you authored in shared workspaces. Artifacts are deleted separately, along with their versions and search entries. Delete or move a workspace's artifacts before deleting the workspace. A URL you shared stays live until you or a remaining owner deletes its artifact.

How long do you keep data?

Artifacts, versions, and comments stay until you delete them. View analytics use a rolling 365-day window and are pruned daily. Unclaimed drafts are deleted after 72 hours. Password reset links last one hour. Access tokens last 24 hours and renew until an account has been inactive for a year. Unfinished agent connections are cleared after 30 days. On a self-hosted instance, you control these retention settings and can turn analytics off.

Can I keep data off the hosted service?

Yes. Derive runs as one container with SQLite and local disk, or with Postgres and S3 at scale. When you self-host, the hosted service providers listed above are not in the request path.

How do I ask a privacy question?

Email hello@derive.to and we will help.
