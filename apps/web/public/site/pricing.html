Details

Billing questions

Who counts as an editor?

Anyone with a Creator or Admin seat during a billing period. Viewers can read and comment without a paid seat. They become billable only when a workspace owner grants Creator or Admin access.

Do agents need paid seats?

No. Agents use the permissions granted by the workspace. Billing is based on people with Creator or Admin access.

What happens if I go over a free limit?

Your workspace becomes read-only. Every artifact stays live at its URL with its version history and comments. Publishing resumes when the workspace is within its limits or moves to a paid plan.

Can I self-host instead?

Yes. Derive runs as one container with SQLite and local disk, or with Postgres and S3 at scale. The hosted plans are for teams that would rather not operate it themselves.

What license does Derive use?

Derive is Fair Source under FSL-1.1-ALv2. You can use, modify, and self-host it for anything except selling Derive itself as a service. Each release converts to Apache-2.0 two years after it ships.

When will billing start?

We have not set a date. We will announce it ahead of time, give existing workspaces a grace period, and keep a free plan.
