The first hour

Do these in order. Skipping ahead to diagnosis is the most common way an incident gets longer.

  1. Acknowledge within five minutes. Silence reads as nobody looking. Even "seen it, digging" stops a second person duplicating your work.
  2. Say what is broken, for users. Not "queue depth rising" but "invoices are not sending". If you cannot phrase it that way, that is the first thing to find out.
  3. Check whether anything shipped. Most incidents follow a change. Look at the last deploy before you look at anything clever.
  4. Restore, then understand. Roll back, fail over, or turn the feature off. A clean diagnosis at 4am is worth less than a working system at 3am.
  5. Write as you go. A running note costs nothing now and is the whole review later. Timestamps matter more than prose.

If you are stuck for twenty minutes

Escalate. Twenty minutes without progress is the threshold, not a sign of weakness. See escalation.
