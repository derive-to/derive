Afterwards

Write it down while it is still fresh. The details that explain the incident are gone by the next morning.

  1. The timeline. When it started, when someone noticed, when it was restored. The gap between the first two is usually the most useful number.
  2. What made it hard. The missing dashboard, the alert that did not fire, the runbook that was wrong. This is the part that prevents the next one.
  3. What you changed under pressure. Anything done by hand needs to be either undone or made permanent on purpose. Write it down before you forget it exists.

Not a blame exercise

The question is what made the mistake easy to make, not who made it. A review that ends in "be more careful" has found nothing.
