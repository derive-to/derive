Escalation

Escalating is a routine action with a threshold, not a judgement about you.

When

  1. Twenty minutes without progress. Not without a fix, without progress.
  2. Anything touching money or customer data. Escalate immediately, in parallel with your own work.
  3. You are about to do something you cannot undo. Second pair of eyes first.

Who

Secondary on-callDefault. Wake them for anything above.
Service ownerWhen the failure is inside one service and the secondary does not know it.
Incident leadWhen it needs coordination or an external message.

What they need in the first message

What is broken for users, when it started, what you have already tried, and what you want from them. Four lines. A paste of the whole log is not a handoff.
