How it works

The stage is fixed. Only the scale changes.

1280 x 720Design against real pixels. One transform fits the stage to any viewport, so nothing reflows and nothing is cropped.
Slides layerEach section sits absolute in the stage. Toggling .on crossfades — no page loads, no scroll.
The host joins inPosting your position turns on Derive's deck bar, Present mode, and per-slide comments.
Make it yours

Every slide earns a picture and a sentence.

Restyle everythingPalette, type, spacing, the transition. Keep the stage, the slide sections, and the two messages.
Add slidesAppend a section with the next data-derive-slide index. Don't renumber the existing ones.
Show, don't listInline SVG, a diagram, a screenshot — a visual that argues something the words don't.
